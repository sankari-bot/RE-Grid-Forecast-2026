Page 4: How it Works

Real-time Renewable Energy Grid Imbalance Forecasting

High Deficit Pattern:

low generation, high load, fossil gas down, coal outage, high temp 35C, evening peak 18-20h

Load > Generation by >1000 MW, Grid needs backup power!

BALANCED Pattern:

generation = load +- 500 MW, rolling average stable, temp 15-25C

Imbalance near 0, Grid stable operation

High EXCESS Pattern:

High solar + wind, low load, sunny midday 11-15h

Generation > Load by >1000 MW, Export / Store in battery!

Data Pipeline:

1. Energy CSV: 35064 hours - biomass, fossil brown coal gas, fossil gas, hard coal, oil, oil shale
2. Weather CSV: 178396 records -> Aggregated hourly -> 35064 hours
3. Merged: FINAL Dataset 35004 rows after cleaning

Tech Stack:

Regression: RandomForestRegressor (50 trees, depth 12)
Classification: RandomForestClassifier for Grid Status

line_plot (Actual vs Pred), bar_plot (Accuracy), pie_chart (Status), confusion (Matrix), scatter (PCA), hist (Imbalance), word_count (Load Curve)

Dataset: 35004 hours = 4 years smart grid data
